💡 What is Ollama? It's a free, open-source tool that downloads, manages, and runs AI models. One command in your terminal and you're chatting with AI. No accounts, no subscriptions, no data leaving your computer.

2 Pick Your Model

Click what kind of hardware you have. We'll recommend the best model for you.

3 Install and Run

Open your terminal (Command Prompt on Windows, Terminal on Mac) and run these commands:

Step 1: Download your model
ollama pull phi-4-mini
Step 2: Start chatting
ollama run phi-4-mini

That's it! Type your questions and the AI responds right in your terminal.

🎯 What to Try First

Sample prompts to try after your model is running:

  • Explain quantum computing in simple terms
  • Write a short story about a robot
  • Give me 5 ideas for a blog post
  • What are the best practices for password security?
💡 Pro tip: Type /help in the chat to see available commands. Type /exit to quit.

🔧 Troubleshooting

  • "Ollama is not recognized" — Close and reopen your terminal after installing. On Windows, search for "Command Prompt" and try again.
  • "Not enough space" — Models are 2-8GB. Check your free disk space. Try a smaller model like ollama pull phi-4-mini (2.5GB).
  • "Out of memory" — Your model is too large for your RAM/VRAM. Close other applications and try a smaller model, or use a quantized version.
  • Model is very slow — CPU-only inference is slower than GPU. This is normal. Use a smaller model for faster responses.
  • "Connection error" — Check your internet connection. You only need internet to download the model. After that, it works fully offline.
  • How do I use a different model? — Run ollama pull [model-name] then ollama run [model-name]. See the Model Compendium for options.
