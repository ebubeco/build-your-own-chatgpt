Frequently Asked Questions

Can RTX 4060 run local AI?

Yes. The RTX 4060 with 8GB VRAM runs 7B models comfortably and handles quantized 13B models.

Is 8GB VRAM enough for local AI?

8GB is enough for most 7B models. You can also run smaller quantized 13B models with reduced context length.

What is the best model for RTX 4060?

Qwen 2.5 7B offers the best balance of speed and quality for the 4060s 8GB VRAM.

Can RTX 4060 run coding models?

Yes. Qwen 2.5 Coder 7B and DeepSeek Coder run great on the RTX 4060 for coding assistance.

Is Ollama free on RTX 4060?

Yes. Ollama is completely free and uses your GPU automatically.
