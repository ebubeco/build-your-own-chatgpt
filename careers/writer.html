Best Local AI Models for Writers

Writers can use local AI for editing, brainstorming, and drafting. Unlike cloud AI, local models keep your writing private, work without internet, and have no usage limits or subscription fees.

Top Picks for Writers

  • Qwen 2.5 7B: Best all-rounder for creative and professional writing. Runs on 16GB RAM.
  • Gemma 3 12B: Excellent for long-form writing, editing, and style analysis.
  • Phi-4-mini 3.8B: Lightweight option for 8GB laptops. Great for quick edits and proofreading.
  • Mistral 7B: Strong creative writing and storytelling capabilities.

How Writers Use Local AI

  • Editing: Improve sentence structure, grammar, and style while keeping your voice.
  • Brainstorming: Generate plot ideas, character concepts, and article outlines.
  • Drafting: Write first drafts of articles, blog posts, and creative pieces.
  • Research: Summarize sources and gather background information for your writing.
