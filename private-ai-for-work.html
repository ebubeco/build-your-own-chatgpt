Local AI means your data never leaves your computer. Unlike ChatGPT or other cloud services, local AI models run entirely on your machine. No API calls, no data logging, no third-party access to your work documents or conversations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is local AI private for work?

Yes, local AI is completely private. All model inference happens on your computer. No data is sent to external servers, no API calls are made, and nothing is logged remotely.

Is local AI GDPR compliant?

Yes, local AI is inherently GDPR compliant. Since all data stays on your device, there is no data transfer, no third-party processing, and no external storage of personal data.

Can I use local AI for confidential business data?

Yes. Local AI is ideal for confidential data. Models run entirely on your computer with no internet connection needed. Your documents, code, and conversations never leave your machine.

How is local AI different from ChatGPT for work?

ChatGPT sends your conversations to OpenAI's servers. Local AI processes everything on your computer. For businesses handling sensitive data, local AI eliminates the risk of data exposure through third-party APIs.
