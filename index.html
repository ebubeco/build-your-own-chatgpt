The choice

Local AI vs. ChatGPT

Both get the job done. But they work very differently.

Local AI — this tool
  • Runs on your machine
  • 100% private, nothing leaves your device
  • One-time setup, free forever
  • Works without internet
  • No usage limits
  • You own the whole stack
ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini
  • Runs on their servers
  • Data may be used for training
  • Requires paid subscription for the good stuff
  • Needs an internet connection
  • Usage caps on free tiers
  • Company controls your workflow
