Frequently Asked Questions

Can I run AI without a GPU?

Yes. Local AI runs on CPU. Small models (1-3B) are functional, while larger models need patience.

Which model is best for CPU-only?

TinyLlama 1.1B offers the best speed-to-quality ratio for CPU-only systems.

Is Ollama free on CPU?

Yes. Ollama is free on all platforms, with or without a GPU.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. If you add a GPU later, Ollama detects it automatically and accelerates inference.
