Do I need Ollama on Mac?

Yes. Ollama has native Apple Silicon support and uses Metal for GPU acceleration automatically.

How much unified memory do I need?

8GB can run 3B models well. 16GB+ is recommended for 7B models and larger.

Is local AI fast on M2?
