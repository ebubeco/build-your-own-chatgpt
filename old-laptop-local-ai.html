Frequently Asked Questions

Can my old laptop run AI?

Yes. If it has at least 4GB RAM, you can run TinyLlama 1.1B and similar small models.

Will local AI drain my battery?

Yes, continuous inference uses CPU heavily. Expect 2-3 hours of runtime on battery.

Which OS supports local AI?

Windows, macOS, and Linux all support Ollama and local AI models.

Can I use local AI without internet?

Yes. After the initial model download, everything runs offline.
